Most people underestimate how many subscriptions they pay for each month. Streaming services, cloud storage, fitness apps, and free trials that auto-renew can quietly add up.

## Why subscription tracking matters

Recurring charges are easy to forget. A trial you meant to cancel, a service you stopped using, or a price hike you missed can cost hundreds per year.

Tracking everything in one app gives you:

- A clear monthly total before charges hit
- Reminders before renewal dates
- Visibility into trials that are about to convert

## Step 1: List every subscription

Start with the obvious ones: Netflix, Spotify, iCloud, and any software you use for work. Then check your email for receipts and your bank or card statements for recurring charges.

Add each service to SubFox with:

- Monthly or yearly cost
- Billing date
- Payment method (optional but helpful)

## Step 2: Set payment reminders

Enable notifications a few days before each charge. That window is enough to cancel, downgrade, or budget for the payment.

For annual plans, set reminders further in advance — those single yearly hits are easy to forget.

## Step 3: Organize by category

Group subscriptions into categories like Entertainment, Productivity, and Health. Categories make it obvious where your money goes and which areas to trim first.

## Step 4: Review monthly

Once a month, open your dashboard and ask:

- Am I still using this service?
- Is there a cheaper plan?
- Did any trial convert without me noticing?

Canceling one unused $9.99/month service saves roughly $120 per year.

## Common mistakes to avoid

**Ignoring free trials.** Always add trials with the conversion date as the billing date.

**Forgetting shared accounts.** Family plans still count — track your share of the cost.

**Only tracking big subscriptions.** Small app subscriptions add up faster than you think.

## FAQ

### How many subscriptions does the average person have?

Studies often cite 10–12 active subscriptions per household, but many people discover more once they audit statements.

### Should I use a spreadsheet instead of an app?

Spreadsheets work for a static list, but apps like SubFox add reminders, categories, and quick updates on your phone — which matters when you are trying to cancel before a renewal.

### Can I track subscriptions offline?

Yes. SubFox stores data locally on your device, so your list is available even without internet.
